# Okinawa Private Tour Brand Assets

Single source for Okinawa Private Tour logo files, color tokens, and usage rules.

## Logo Files

- Original PNG: `brand/logo/okinawa-private-tour-original.png`
- Horizontal transparent PNG: `brand/logo/okinawa-private-tour-horizontal.png`
- Square avatar PNG: `brand/logo/okinawa-private-tour-avatar.png`
- White transparent PNG for dark backgrounds: `brand/logo/okinawa-private-tour-white.png`
- Favicon 32: `brand/logo/favicon-32.png`
- Apple touch icon 180: `brand/logo/apple-touch-icon-180.png`
- App icon 512: `brand/logo/icon-512.png`

SVG was not produced in this pass. The local machine had no tracing tools such as `potrace`, `autotrace`, or `inkscape`, and this watercolor hand-drawn logo should not be manually approximated as a fake vector.

## Clearspace

Keep open space around the logo on all four sides. Minimum clearspace is one spiral diameter from the avatar mark. Do not let text, buttons, photos, or page edges enter this area.

## Minimum Size

- Horizontal logo, digital: at least 160 px wide.
- Horizontal logo, print: at least 35 mm wide.
- Avatar mark, digital: at least 48 px square.
- Favicon: use the prepared 32 px file only.

## Do Not

- Do not stretch or squeeze the logo.
- Do not recolor the logo.
- Do not add drop shadows, glows, outlines, or filters.
- Do not put the full-color logo on busy photos.
- Do not use yellow as a button background.
- On dark backgrounds, use the white version.

## Subline Format

Use this structure for activity sub-brands:

`Fishing Okinawa / Snorkel Okinawa — by Okinawa Private Tour`

## Color Rules

- `--opt-blue-900` is the primary brand color for headings and primary buttons.
- `--opt-blue-500` is for links and secondary emphasis.
- `--opt-teal-500` is for accents and tags.
- `--opt-mint-500` is for success and `NO CHARGE` style labels.
- `--opt-sky-200` is for light dividers and soft backgrounds.
- `--opt-sun-500` is only for small accent marks. Do not use it as a button fill.
- Cards use white background, `1px #DCE8EC` border, 12 px radius, and a light shadow.
- Dangerous or destructive actions use neutral gray, not red.
